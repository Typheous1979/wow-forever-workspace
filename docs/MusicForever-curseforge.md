# MusicForever

**A jukebox for every piece of music in World of Warcraft Forever, plus a full game audio mixer.**

Play any zone, city, dungeon or raid theme wherever you are, and hand the music back to the game with one click.

## Features

### Music library
- **747 tracks**, organized the way the world is:
  - **Zones** › Eastern Kingdoms / Kalimdor / Zephras Isle › zone › Day / Night / Intro, with subzones that have their own music;
  - **Cities**, including districts and taverns;
  - **Dungeons**, **Raids**, **Battlegrounds**;
  - **Music Sets** and **Other Music**.
- **Search** finds tracks and folders at every level.
- **Forever's new and remastered music** is marked, next to the original 2004 tracks.

### Player
- **Controls:** double-click to play; **Play all**, **Shuffle**, **Repeat** (all / one / off), Previous / Next, and a progress bar.
- **Return to game music:** fades out and gives the music back to the zone.
- **Optional auto-return:** when you enter a dungeon, change zone or enter combat.

### Playlists
- **Favorites** (one click on the star) and your own **playlists**, shared by all your characters.

### Mixer
- **Volume:** every sound channel the game offers (Master, Music, Effects, Ambience, Dialog, Gameplay Sounds, Combat Warnings, Pings), each with on/off and volume.
- **Sound options:** Loop Music, Sound in Background, Reverb, Distance Filtering, Hear from Character, Error Speech, Emote and Pet sounds.
- **Presets:** Blizzard Default, Music Focus, Immersive, Quiet, Raid Focus and Game Music Off, plus **your own presets** and **Undo**.

### Everywhere you want it
- **Key bindings:** open/close, play/stop, next, previous, return to game music, music and master volume up/down, game music on/off, next preset, mini player.
- **Minimap button:** works with MinimapButtonButton and similar collectors.
- **Addon compartment entry**.
- **Mini player:** a small movable now-playing bar.
- **Slash command:** `/mf` (`/mf help` for the rest).

## How it works
MusicForever plays the game's own music files; it contains no audio. Its catalog is built from Forever's game data and checked in game, so it lists only music that plays on Forever.
